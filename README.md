## Sales Report :


- **Project objective:** 

    **1.** Create a _[customer performance report](https://github.com/Srikanth-reddy-123/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)_ 

    **2.** Conduct a comprehensive comparison between _[market performance and sales targets](https://github.com/Srikanth-reddy-123/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf)_

- **Purpose of sales analytics:** Empower AtliQ Hardwares to monitor, evaluate, and scale its global sales activities and account performance across 23 countries.

- **Importance of analyzing sales data:** Identify regional sales patterns, track key performance indicators (KPIs) like YoY net sales growth (+204.5% in FY2021), and monitor customer growth trends (e.g., Amazon reaching ₹82.1M).

- **Role of reports:** Determine effective customer discounts, facilitate negotiations with major retail partners (e.g., AtliQ Exclusive, Flipkart), and identify global target gaps (-₹54.9M shortfall) to guide expansion in high-potential markets like India, USA, and South Korea.


## Finance Report :

- **Project objective:** 

    **1.** Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by _[Fiscal Year](https://github.com/Srikanth-reddy-123/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Fiscal%20Year.pdf)_ & _[Months](https://github.com/Srikanth-reddy-123/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Months.pdf)_ 

   **2.** Create Profit and Loss (P&L) reports by _[Markets](https://github.com/Srikanth-reddy-123/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Markets.pdf)_

- **Purpose of sales analytics:** Evaluation of overall financial health, tracking Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), measuring Gross Margin percentages across fiscal cycles, and facilitating communication with executive stakeholders.

- **Importance of analyzing Finance data:** Aid in benchmarking product profitability across regions (e.g., New Zealand leading at 48.2% GM vs. Germany at 26.2% GM), evaluating seasonal revenue surges (e.g., Q1/Q2 peaks in Nov–Dec), and establishing a reliable foundation for corporate budgeting and forecasting.

- **Role of reports:** Align multi-region financial planning with strategic growth targets, protect profit margins during volume expansion, and instill stakeholder confidence in AtliQ Hardwares' financial trajectory.
